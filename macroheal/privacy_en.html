3. Use Purposes

  • Account creation and authentication

We never sell, rent, or share your data for advertising.

4. Security

  • Data encrypted with AES-256 on Firebase
  • Local data in device's secure storage
  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt
  • All server traffic over TLS 1.2+

5. Retention

  • Active account: Retained while active
  • Inactive account: Anonymized after 24 months of inactivity
  • Account deletion: Permanently deleted within 30 days of request
